# 1. Purpose

This journey defines how advertisers and ecosystem partners engage with Localisy.

# 2. Advertiser Journey

1. understand available ad products
2. create or submit campaign
3. define targeting, creatives, budget, and schedule
4. receive approval or revision feedback
5. monitor campaign analytics and outcomes
6. optimize, renew, or expand spend

# 3. Partner Journey

1. share data, listings, or locality collaboration inputs
2. align on quality and governance rules
3. onboard through controlled intake or API/file process
4. monitor coverage and issue resolution

# 4. Success Measures

- advertiser retention
- campaign outcome quality
- partner data quality
- lower ops friction in approval and support flows
