# 1. Core Rule

In Localisy, the tenant is the locality.

# 2. Implications

- locality is the operational scope for listings, routing, homepage behavior, and many admin workflows
- city is an aggregation layer, not the primary tenant
- national is a discovery umbrella, not the operational tenant

# 3. Why This Model Fits

- the product is hyperlocal
- user intent is typically neighborhood or locality based
- merchant competition and sponsored placement are locality sensitive
- SEO landing pages map naturally to locality intent
