# 1. Purpose

This document defines who owns, maintains, and approves critical data inside Localisy.

# 2. Ownership Areas

- geography master data
- listing master data
- taxonomy and classification
- reviews and trust signals
- campaign and ad data
- legal and policy content
- analytics definitions

# 3. Stewardship Model

- product owns scope and intent
- ops owns quality and operational correctness
- engineering owns schemas, controls, and technical integrity
- compliance owns policy-sensitive definitions
- growth owns SEO and acquisition configuration

# 4. Decision Rules

- canonical records should have a clear owner
- data changes affecting trust or compliance should be auditable
- bulk changes should require stronger review than routine edits
