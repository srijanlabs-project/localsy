# 1. Purpose

This document defines how Localisy should balance locality tenancy with city-wide and national discovery surfaces.

# 2. Experience Layers

- locality page as the primary trust and conversion surface
- city page as the aggregation and comparison layer
- national page as the broad discovery and brand layer

# 3. Core Rules

- tenancy remains locality-based
- city and national pages aggregate shared-database content
- the same entity should not be recreated across surfaces
- locality relevance should remain strongest in results and recommendations

# 4. Operational Implication

- SEO, routing, analytics, and navigation should understand all three layers
- operators should manage locality quality without breaking city and national aggregation
