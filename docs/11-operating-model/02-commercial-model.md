# 1. Who Pays

- merchant businesses
- advertisers
- possibly locality or city operators in later models

# 2. What They Pay For

- claimed or enhanced listings
- sponsored positioning
- lead generation
- premium SEO and visibility features
- promotion and campaign tools

# 3. Commercial Design Principle

Monetization should enhance visibility without reducing buyer trust.
