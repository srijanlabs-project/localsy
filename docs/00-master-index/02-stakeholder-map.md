# 1. Purpose

This document defines the primary stakeholder groups that the Localisy documentation library must serve.

# 2. Core Stakeholders

- buyers and local residents
- merchants and advertisers
- locality operators
- moderation and support teams
- growth and SEO teams
- product managers
- UX and design teams
- frontend, backend, AI, and data engineers
- QA teams
- legal, compliance, and governance teams

# 3. Stakeholder Goals

- buyers want fast, trustworthy local discovery
- merchants want visibility, leads, reviews, and advertising outcomes
- advertisers want measurable campaign performance and spend control
- operators want efficient listing, moderation, and locality operations
- product and design teams want a clear source of truth
- engineering and QA teams want implementation-ready clarity

# 4. Documentation Implications

- every critical workflow should identify its primary actor
- every high-risk capability should show ops, compliance, and audit treatment
- every growth-critical feature should include analytics and success metrics
- every buyer-facing flow should preserve clarity, trust, and low friction
