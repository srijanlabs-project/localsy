# 1. Purpose

Define how Localisy should model ad pricing, billing, pacing, and advertiser-facing commercial rules.

# 2. Supported Commercial Models

- fixed placement pricing
- CPC pricing
- lead-based pricing where supported
- managed campaign pricing
- promotional bundles for small and home-based businesses

# 3. Pricing Inputs

- locality demand
- page type
- placement slot
- category competitiveness
- duration
- targeting depth
- creative count
- managed-service involvement

# 4. Billing Rules

- pricing rules should be versioned
- advertiser invoices should reflect placement, date range, and billing model
- spend caps should be enforceable
- paused or rejected campaigns should not accrue invalid charges

# 5. Advertiser Visibility

Advertisers should be able to see:

- planned spend
- actual spend
- click or lead counts
- remaining budget
- active schedule
- current creative mix

# 6. Ops and Finance Controls

- pricing overrides should be auditable
- manual credits and debits should be restricted
- campaign approval and billing state should remain reconcilable

# 7. Dependencies

- Offers, Ads, and Promotion
- Billing and Commercial
- Analytics and Reporting
- Admin Operations
