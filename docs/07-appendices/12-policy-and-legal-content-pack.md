# 1. Purpose

This appendix lists the policy and legal content packages Localisy must publish and maintain.

# 2. Public Legal Pages

- terms and conditions
- privacy policy
- cookie policy
- disclaimer

# 3. Commercial and Merchant Policies

- seller agreement
- refund policy
- cancellation policy
- fulfilment policy
- advertising policy

# 4. Platform Governance Policies

- community guidelines
- listing policy
- review policy
- moderation policy
- grievance and takedown policy

# 5. Control Requirements

- every policy should support versioning
- acceptance tracking should be enabled where required
- changes should be auditable
- geo-specific variants should be supported when needed
