# 1. Domain Purpose

This domain covers merchant acquisition, listing ownership, lead generation, and commercial monetization.

# 2. Core Capabilities

- merchant onboarding
- merchant workspace
- subscriptions and visibility tiers
- promotions and ads
- lead and CRM workflows

# 3. Main Business Questions

- how do merchants join or claim their place on Localisy?
- how do merchants pay for more visibility?
- how does the platform prove merchant value?
