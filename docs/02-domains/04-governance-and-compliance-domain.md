# 1. Domain Purpose

This domain covers the controls that keep Localisy safe, auditable, privacy-aware, and legally usable.

# 2. Core Capabilities

- moderation
- audit logging
- privacy controls
- legal policies
- consent handling
- grievance and takedown workflows

# 3. Main Business Questions

- how does the platform stay trustworthy?
- how are sensitive actions and data controlled?
- how are complaints, rights requests, and policy rules handled?
