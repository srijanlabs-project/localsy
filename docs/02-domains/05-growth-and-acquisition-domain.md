# 1. Purpose

This domain defines how Localisy acquires users, merchants, and advertiser demand.

# 2. Scope

- SEO acquisition
- locality launch playbooks
- category expansion strategy
- merchant acquisition funnels
- advertiser acquisition and retention
- campaign measurement and feedback loops

# 3. Domain Outcomes

- more discovery traffic
- more claimed and improved listings
- more advertiser participation
- stronger locality density and trust

# 4. Key Dependencies

- SEO and Organic Growth
- Homepage and CMS
- Merchant Management
- Offers, Ads, and Promotion
- Analytics and Reporting
