# 1. Operating Model

Localisy operates as a locality-scoped discovery platform with shared infrastructure and centralized governance.

# 2. Tenant Model

- primary operational tenant: locality
- aggregation layer: city
- umbrella discovery layer: national

# 3. Primary Personas

## 3.1 Buyer

Uses Localisy to find businesses, compare options, unlock contact details, and review merchants.

## 3.2 Seller or Merchant

Uses Localisy to create or claim listings, improve visibility, receive leads, and run offers.

## 3.3 Operator

Maintains structured data, geography, and listing quality for assigned scopes.

## 3.4 Moderator

Approves or rejects listings, reviews, and flagged content.

## 3.5 Admin

Owns governance, analytics, routing, monetization controls, and platform-wide configuration.

## 3.6 Growth Operator

Owns SEO, campaigns, and traffic growth performance.

# 4. Revenue Paths

- merchant subscriptions
- sponsored listings
- listing ads and lead-form ads
- premium SEO or visibility features

# 5. Key Operating Loops

- acquire buyers through SEO and direct traffic
- convert buyers to contacts and leads
- convert businesses to claimed or paid merchant accounts
- maintain quality through moderation and data governance
