# 1. Product Vision

Localisy is a locality-first hyperlocal business discovery platform that helps users find nearby businesses, compare trusted options, and contact the right merchant quickly across Web and WhatsApp.

# 2. Core Problem

Local commerce discovery is fragmented, unverified, and hard to navigate. Buyers often know the service they need but cannot easily find the right local provider with confidence.

# 3. Product Promise

Localisy should provide:

- locality-aware discovery
- trustworthy listings
- strong search and SEO acquisition
- merchant visibility and lead capture
- scalable operational control for admins and operators

# 4. In-Scope Platform Pillars

- locality and pincode routing
- business listing discovery
- merchant onboarding and listing management
- reviews, trust, and moderation
- promotions, sponsored listings, and leads
- SEO-driven acquisition
- future AI-assisted discovery and WhatsApp support

# 5. Out-of-Scope for Current First Draft

- full marketplace transactions
- logistics or delivery orchestration
- deep ERP or POS integrations
- full native mobile implementation

# 6. Success Outcomes

- buyers find relevant businesses faster
- merchants receive qualified leads
- localities scale without manual page-by-page operations
- the platform stays trustworthy and operationally manageable
