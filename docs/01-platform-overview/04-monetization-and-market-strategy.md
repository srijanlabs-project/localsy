# 1. Purpose

This document defines how Localisy creates commercial value while preserving trust in hyperlocal discovery.

# 2. Core Revenue Lines

- merchant visibility plans
- sponsored listings
- display and lead-form advertising
- premium SEO visibility and richer listing surfaces
- managed campaign services for advertisers

# 3. Strategic Position

Localisy should operate as a locality-first discovery platform, not just a listing repository.

Its market position depends on:

- better locality relevance than broad directories
- stronger trust and moderation than informal community groups
- better advertiser measurability than offline local promotion

# 4. Commercial Principles

- paid visibility must remain clearly labeled
- monetization must not damage search trust
- small home-based businesses should have a viable growth path
- public-service and civic discovery quality must remain protected

# 5. Go-to-Market Focus

- locality-by-locality rollout
- category depth before category breadth
- merchant acquisition through listing value first
- upsell through trust, traffic, analytics, and lead outcomes
